Forex: Market Structure & Margin Trading

While we touched on the basics of Forex in Course 01, this chapter dives deeper into the mechanics of the FX market, which is crucial for configuring your trading algorithms.

Market Sessions

Unlike the stock market, Forex is open 24 hours a day, 5 days a week. However, volatility varies depending on which global market session is active:

  • Sydney & Tokyo (Asian Session): Usually lower volatility. Great for range-trading bots.
  • London (European Session): High volatility and tight spreads. Trends often begin here.
  • New York (US Session): Massive liquidity, especially when it overlaps with the London session (8 AM - 12 PM EST). Breakout bots thrive here.

Pips & Lots

In Forex, price movements are measured in Pips (Percentage in Point). For most pairs, a pip is the 4th decimal place (e.g., a move from 1.1000 to 1.1001 is 1 pip).

Trade sizes are measured in Lots:

  • Standard Lot: 100,000 units of the base currency. (1 pip move ≈ $10).
  • Mini Lot: 10,000 units. (1 pip move ≈ $1).
  • Micro Lot: 1,000 units. (1 pip move ≈ $0.10).

Margin and Leverage

Because currency movements are so small, traders use Leverage. If your broker offers 1:100 leverage, you can control a $100,000 position with just $1,000 in your account. The $1,000 is your Margin.

Warning for Bot Developers: When coding your bot, ALWAYS hardcode a strict Stop Loss and position sizing limit. A bug in a highly leveraged bot can liquidate an account in seconds!
