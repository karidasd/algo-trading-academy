Gateway to Trading: Forex Basics & Moving Averages

Welcome to the first official module of the DarkAIs Academy. In this chapter, we will lay the foundation of your trading career by covering the absolute basics of the Foreign Exchange (Forex) market. Moreover, we will introduce a fundamental algorithmic trading concept: Mean Reversion via Moving Averages.

1. What is Forex?

The Foreign Exchange market (Forex or FX) is the global marketplace for exchanging national currencies. With a daily trading volume exceeding $6 trillion, it is by far the largest and most liquid financial market in the world.

Currencies are important because they allow us to purchase goods and services locally and across borders. In Forex, you always trade in pairs. For example, in the pair EUR/USD:

  • EUR (Euro): This is the Base Currency.
  • USD (US Dollar): This is the Quote Currency.

If EUR/USD is priced at 1.1000, it means 1 Euro is equal to 1.10 US Dollars. When you "Buy" EUR/USD, you are effectively buying Euros and selling US Dollars simultaneously, expecting the Euro to gain value.

"The goal of a successful trader is to make the best trades. Money is secondary." — Alexander Elder

2. The Strategy: Mean Reversion

While human traders often rely on intuition, algorithmic bots rely on math. One of the most common algorithmic strategies is based on Mean Reversion—the idea that prices tend to return to their historical average over time.

In this lesson, we will program our bot to:

  • Calculate a short-term Simple Moving Average (SMA) of the EUR/USD price.
  • If the current price drops significantly below the average, the asset is considered "oversold", and the bot executes a BUY.
  • If the price rises significantly above the average, the asset is considered "overbought", and the bot executes a SELL.
