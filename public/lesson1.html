Gateway to Trading: Forex Basics & Moving Averages

Welcome to the first lesson of the DarkAIs Academy. In this chapter, we will cover the absolute basics of the Foreign Exchange (Forex) market and introduce a fundamental algorithmic trading concept: Mean Reversion via Moving Averages.

What is Forex?

The Forex market is where currencies are traded. Currencies are important because they allow us to purchase goods and services locally and across borders. International currencies need to be exchanged to conduct foreign trade and business.

In Forex, you always trade pairs. For example, EUR/USD represents how many US Dollars (the quote currency) are needed to purchase one Euro (the base currency).

The Strategy: Mean Reversion

One of the most common algorithmic strategies is based on the idea that prices tend to return to their historical average. In this lesson, we will program our bot to:

  • Calculate a short-term moving average of the EUR/USD price.
  • If the current price drops significantly below the average, we assume it's "oversold" and execute a BUY.
  • If the price rises significantly above the average, we assume it's "overbought" and execute a SELL.
