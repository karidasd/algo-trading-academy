Technical Analysis: Support & Resistance

Welcome to the Technical Analysis chapter. Today we focus on one of the most powerful concepts in trading: Support and Resistance.

What are Support and Resistance?

Imagine a bouncing ball in a room. It hits the floor and bounces up; it hits the ceiling and bounces down.

  • Support (The Floor): A price level where a downtrend tends to pause due to a concentration of demand (buying interest).
  • Resistance (The Ceiling): A price level where an uptrend tends to pause due to a concentration of supply (selling interest).

The Strategy: Range Trading

In algorithmic trading, we can program a bot to identify these "invisible floors and ceilings" by looking at the highest highs and lowest lows over a period.

Our strategy rules for the bot:

  • If the price drops near the Support level, execute a BUY, expecting it to bounce up.
  • If the price rises near the Resistance level, execute a SELL, expecting it to drop down.
