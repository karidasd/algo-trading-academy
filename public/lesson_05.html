Fundamental Analysis: News & Economics

While Technical Analysis focuses on the chart, Fundamental Analysis looks at the underlying forces that drive the economy. It is the study of macroeconomic data, central bank policies, and geopolitical events.

Key Economic Indicators

  • Interest Rates: The most important driver in Forex. If a Central Bank raises rates, their currency usually strengthens because it offers higher returns to investors.
  • NFP (Non-Farm Payrolls): Released on the first Friday of every month in the US. It measures job creation and causes massive volatility across all USD pairs.
  • CPI (Consumer Price Index): The primary measure of inflation. High inflation often forces Central Banks to raise interest rates.

Fundamental Bots (News Trading)

Can a bot trade the news? Absolutely. "News Sniper" bots are connected to rapid news APIs (like Bloomberg or Reuters). The millisecond a data point (like NFP) is released, the bot compares it to the forecasted value.

If the actual data is significantly better than expected, the bot instantly buys the currency. If it's worse, it sells. These bots require extremely low latency (often hosted on servers right next to the broker's servers) because humans cannot react fast enough.
