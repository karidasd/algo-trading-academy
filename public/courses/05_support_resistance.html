Support, Resistance & Institutional Order Blocks

Discover how institutional market makers place orders around key price levels and learn to build an algorithmic Range Trading Bot.

1. The Mechanics of Support & Resistance

Support and Resistance are not arbitrary lines on a chart—they are historical clusters of unfilled institutional limit orders. When price approaches these zones:

  • Support (Demand Floor): Massive cluster of Buy Limit orders. Sellers run out of steam, and buying volume drives price upwards.
  • Resistance (Supply Ceiling): Massive cluster of Sell Limit orders. Buyers are exhausted, causing price to roll over.
