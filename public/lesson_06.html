Statistical & Sentiment Analysis

To truly gain an edge in algorithmic trading, you must go beyond standard technical indicators and look at the raw data and the psychology of the crowd.

Statistical Analysis (Quant Trading)

Statistical analysis involves using historical data to find probabilities. Quantitative (Quant) bots don't care about "trendlines." They care about math.

  • Correlation: If Gold goes up, does AUD/USD go up too? A stat-bot will constantly measure this correlation and trade the lag.
  • Monte Carlo Simulations: Running a strategy through thousands of randomized market conditions to ensure the bot survives worst-case scenarios.

Sentiment Analysis

Sentiment is how the majority of traders feel about an asset. Are they fearful or greedy?

In Forex, brokers often publish "Client Positioning" data (e.g., 85% of retail traders are Long on EUR/USD). Because the majority of retail traders lose money (the 90/90/90 rule), smart bots use this data as a contrarian indicator. If 85% of retail is buying, the bot will look for opportunities to SELL.

Furthermore, advanced AI bots scrape Twitter (X) and financial news using Natural Language Processing (NLP) to gauge whether the global mood is bullish or bearish.
