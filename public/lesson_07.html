Trading Platforms & API Integration

Your trading strategy is only as good as the platform executing it. In this chapter, we explore how bots actually talk to brokers.

MetaTrader 4/5 (MT4 & MT5)

The industry standard for Forex. Bots here are called Expert Advisors (EAs) and are written in MQL4 or MQL5 (similar to C++). They live directly on the chart and have zero latency to the terminal.

cTrader

A modern alternative to MT4. Bots are called cBots and are written in C#. cTrader is known for its excellent API and clean execution.

REST APIs & WebSockets (Node.js / Python)

If you don't want to use MQL or C#, you can build your bot in Python or Node.js (like we do at DarkAIs Academy). You connect to the broker using:

  • REST API: You send an HTTP request (like a web browser) saying "Buy 1 Lot of EUR/USD".
  • WebSockets (WSS): A continuous open connection. The broker streams live prices to your bot every millisecond, and your bot can instantly fire back orders.

When using custom APIs, you usually host your bot on a VPS (Virtual Private Server) in London or New York to be physically close to the broker's servers, minimizing ping (latency).
