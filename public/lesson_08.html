Putting It All Together: Your Automated System

Congratulations. You understand the markets, technicals, fundamentals, and the infrastructure. Now it's time to build the complete, deployable automated trading system.

The Architecture of a Pro Bot

  1. The Data Ingestion Layer: A WebSocket connection receiving live ticks, plus a REST API pulling the daily economic calendar (to avoid trading during NFP).
  2. The Alpha / Strategy Layer: Where your math happens. Calculating the SMA, identifying Support/Resistance, checking sentiment.
  3. The Risk Management Engine (Crucial): Before any trade is fired, this layer asks: "Do we have enough margin? Is the spread too high? Are we risking more than 1% of the account?" If no, the trade is blocked.
  4. The Execution Layer: Sends the order to the broker and manages the Trailing Stop Loss.
  5. The Dashboard (Like DarkAIs): A web UI to monitor the bot's performance from your phone.

Final Advice

Never deploy a bot to a live account without running it on a Demo/Paper account for at least a month. The market will always behave in ways you didn't program for. A bot is not a "set and forget" money printer; it is a tool that requires constant monitoring, tweaking, and respect for risk.
