Mastering Prop Firm Challenges: FTMO Rules & Evaluation

Learn how proprietary trading firms like FTMO evaluate talent, the strict mathematical constraints of Maximum Daily Loss vs Max Total Drawdown, and how to configure bots to pass evaluation phases.

1. How Prop Trading Evaluations Work (FTMO Model)

Proprietary trading firms allocate funded accounts (ranging from $10,000 up to $200,000+) to skilled traders who prove consistency through a 2-step evaluation process:

Evaluation Metric FTMO Standard Rule Algorithmic Safeguard
Profit Target +10% (Phase 1) / +5% (Phase 2) Consistent 1:2 R:R trades
Maximum Daily Loss -5% of starting day equity Hard circuit-breaker stop at -3.5%
Maximum Total Loss -10% overall drawdown Cap aggregate active open risk to 2.5%

2. The Number One Reason Traders Fail Prop Challenges

Over 88% of failed challenge attempts are caused by violating the Maximum Daily Loss rule—typically during a single volatile trading session where the trader chased losses. By automating your strategy with hard-coded circuit breakers, human emotion is removed and the rules are respected 100% of the time.
